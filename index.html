About Me

Architecting High Performance Apps

Combining engineering rigor with pixel-perfect design aesthetics.

I'm a mobile developer who builds Flutter and Jetpack Compose applications that are as performant under the hood as they are delightful on screen. My work sits at the intersection of engineering and design: I obsess over system architecture and clean, scalable code, but I never lose sight of the person who'll actually be tapping through the app.

I believe great mobile experiences come from getting the small things right smooth animations, intuitive flows, and interfaces that feel effortless backed by architecture that holds up as products grow. Beyond shipping features, I'm constantly experimenting with new tools and patterns in the Flutter and Compose ecosystems to keep my craft sharp. What drives me isn't just solving technical problems it's building applications people genuinely enjoy using.
